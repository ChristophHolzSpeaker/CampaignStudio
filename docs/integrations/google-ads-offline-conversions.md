# Google Ads offline conversions

Campaign Studio uses these Google Ads conversion actions for server-side outcomes in Ads customer `2354667197`.

|   # | Conversion name                       | ID           | Type            | Category           | Level     | Label | When Campaign Studio invokes it                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Server source                                                                                                                                                              |
| --: | ------------------------------------- | ------------ | --------------- | ------------------ | --------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | CS \| Booking Request Email Qualified | `7755647198` | `UPLOAD_CLICKS` | `SUBMIT_LEAD_FORM` | Primary   | —     | When the Gmail worker receives an email and classifies it as a speaking inquiry. This records `lead_qualified`, which becomes the `booking_request_email_qualified` conversion.                                                      | `worker/src/lib/gmail/process-inbound-message.ts`                                                                                                                          |
|   2 | CS \| Booking Calendar Confirmed      | `7755799332` | `UPLOAD_CLICKS` | `BOOK_APPOINTMENT` | Primary   | —     | When someone confirms a booking through their lead booking link and Campaign Studio has successfully created the Google Calendar event. This records `booking_completed`, which becomes the `booking_calendar_confirmed` conversion. | `src/routes/book/l/[token]/+page.server.ts` and `src/lib/components/booking/LeadInlineBookingSequence.remote.ts`                                                           |
|   3 | CS \| AI Assistant Email Response     | `7755799335` | `UPLOAD_CLICKS` | `DEFAULT`          | Secondary | —     | When Woody successfully sends its AI-assisted reply to the lead. Drafting a reply is not enough: the worker records `autoresponse_sent` only after the send succeeds, which becomes the `ai_assistant_email_response` conversion.    | `worker/src/lib/inbound/run-autoresponse.ts`                                                                                                                               |
|   4 | CS \| Lead Marked Eligible (manual)   | `7755799338` | `UPLOAD_CLICKS` | `QUALIFIED_LEAD`   | Primary   | —     | When a human explicitly clicks **Mark eligible** in the analytics dashboard. The dashboard backend posts `lead_marked_eligible` to Campaign Studio. Page views and automatic enrichment do not invoke it.                            | `src/routes/api/public/v1/lead-journeys/[id]/outcomes/+server.ts`, with `src/routes/api/public/v1/campaign-visits/[id]/outcomes/+server.ts` as the explicit-visit fallback |

The first three rows start from durable server events and are converted by the scheduled reconciler in `src/lib/server/tracking/native-outcomes.ts`. The fourth arrives through the public outcome API. All four use the default event-to-action mapping in `src/lib/tracking/native-outcomes.ts`; a campaign-specific offline mapping can override a default.

Before uploading, Campaign Studio requires an attributable Google click and recorded consent. It sends eligible conversions through the Google Data Manager API and uses stable event IDs to avoid duplicates. These are server-side conversions, so the Google Ads label is blank and the dashboard must not also fire them through GTM.
